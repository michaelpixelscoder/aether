# Voxel Shading

## Starting point

We actually have a basic voxel system, with basic texture and shading.
But we want to go way further.

You can look at the concept designs in design/initial_researches and design/skyship-building-tool 

## Problem or opportunity

We don't want a voxel engine similar to micraft visually. We want something richer. With less tiling, more continuous surfaces, and a more natural appearance.


## Hypothesis

I think the way space engineers handle connected armor and larger macro textures could be a good inspiration for achieving more continuous and natural-looking surfaces in our voxel engine.

## Exploration

You can look at the brainstorming session with ChatGPT at thoughts/voxel-shading/voxel-shading.brainstorm.md

## Assumptions

- A voxel engine similar to space engineers one would be enough to support more continuous and natural-looking surfaces while maintaining a voxel-based simulation.


## Next experiment

We should make a small implementation on wood to test how macro textures and connected surfaces can improve the visual quality while keeping the voxel-based simulation intact.
